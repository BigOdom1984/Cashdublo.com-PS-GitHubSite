CashDublo accounts are not switched on yet. When they are — one sitting at the server, nothing on this page to rebuild — every surface below wakes up with your own account: the settings save, the password form works, your memberships show, and your apps' settings stand here. Until that day the forms stand disabled rather than pretend to save anything.

Account settings

The name and address your account answers to, shown the same way on every CashDublo surface.

Change your password

Just like every other website: sign in, set a new password here, and it changes for the whole account — the store, the App Store app, and every surface that signs in with it. The change runs through the account provider itself; this page never holds your old password, and no password is ever typed into a page that is not the provider's own door.

Accounts that sign in with Google or Apple keep their password with that provider — there is no CashDublo password to change, and this panel will say so for those accounts instead of offering a form that does nothing.

Membership access

Memberships belong to apps, and this account is where yours will stand. When an app offers a membership and you hold it, it shows here — what it opens, when it renews, and its standing — beside the app's own price terms, which are set per app and stated on its store page and in the pricing and memberships guide.

What a membership opens is the premium side of its app. Where an app offers it, a membership paid for through the app itself counts here too: sign into this account, and the same membership opens that app's premium side on the other surfaces — so a membership bought on the phone is honored on the web, and the other way around. Premium access is an entitlement the server checks, never a switch a page flips on its own word.

No memberships stand on any account today, because accounts are not switched on yet. Nothing here invents a price: each app's membership terms are its own, set in the App Catalog and shown before anyone pays.

Settings for your apps

Some apps carry settings that belong to you, not to one device — the way the app greets you, the units it counts in, the look it wears. Those settings will stand here, one app at a time, changeable from the website: pick the app, change the setting, and the app reads the change through the CashDublo server the next time it checks in — the same value on the phone, the tablet, and the web.

The picker fills from the same App Catalog the store shelves read. An app's settings appear here only when that app declares them for the account — each app names its own account settings in its own record, and the website offers exactly those, never a setting the app has not declared. This surface wakes with the same switch-on as the rest of the page; the contract it will speak — the shape of an app's settings, how the app reads them, and how a change made here reaches the app — stands written in the store's record (store/account-contract.md).
