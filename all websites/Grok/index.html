Greg Odom · Big Odom
The person behind the pixels

Built from wherever life takes me.

I'm Greg Odom — independent app developer, founder of CashDublo, and the one pair of hands behind every app on this site. I take an idea from a sketch to an app people can hold: design, code, testing, release, and everything after.

My degree is in Interdisciplinary Studies from UNLV. Computer science I learned the hard way — by building. I served in the United States Air Force. Home base is now Cambodia; I build while traveling Southeast Asia. Offline-first and multi-language support (including Khmer, Lao, Thai) are not afterthoughts.

I also make music as Big Odom. Software and music are two ways of the same thing: making something real that wasn't there before.
