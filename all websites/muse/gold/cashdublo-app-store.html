About this store — the platform behind the shelves

A platform for developers to present their apps, distribute approved builds, manage customer purchases and subscriptions, handle licensing, and deliver updates through one coordinated service. The shelves above are that platform's catalogue, live on the web.

App catalogue

Product pages, app metadata, platform compatibility, release channels and approved downloads.

Commerce and licensing

Distribution and updates

Version manifests, package integrity checks, staged releases, device enrollment and update delivery.

Product-specific pricing

The App Store is the catalogue, checkout, licensing and delivery layer. It does not impose a separate platform membership just to browse or purchase an app. Each product owns its own price and customer entitlement: app memberships belong to apps; engine and studio licences belong to their development platforms; published games and web apps own their own purchase or access terms.

See the CashDublo commercial architecture and pricing guide for product ownership, proposed prices and items whose prices have not yet been set.

Security and customer protections

Signing operations are handled only through the designated protected service. This public page intentionally contains no private signing material or operational key details. Customer access is controlled by documented entitlements, notices, grace periods and appeal procedures. Local customer data must not be destroyed or held hostage when a hosted entitlement is suspended.

Lawful criticism, negative reviews and complaints are not grounds for suspension or fees. Enforcement is limited to genuine material breaches such as fraud, security abuse or unlawful use, under clear published terms.

Availability

This page is the storefront; purchases complete through the CashDublo server shelf. Do not treat a listing as purchasable until the platform, payment processing, licensing, support terms and release status have been verified for it.
